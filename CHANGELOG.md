# Nitro Wheelz change history

## [0.1.0] — Driving Lab (work in progress)
- Initialized from Karll Game Starter v0.1.0 (Godot 4.7.2).
- Implemented original planar two-axle car physics inspired by DRIFTIT developer-published principles.
- Procedural fictional Japanese circuit Shin-Kawa Drift Park, custom low-poly RWD prototype car and tyre marks.
- Default overhead and optional chase cameras, 3 laps, drift score and responsive HUD.
- Multi-finger touch controls, Xbox controller mapping and keyboard inputs.
- Determinism and physics regression tests plus starter smoke/quality checks.
- Not yet physically tested on Android, Windows or Xbox hardware. No signed installable APK is claimed.
