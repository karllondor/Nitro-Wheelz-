# NITRO WHEELZ — game brief (approved direction)

## One-sentence vision
Japanese drift nights, demanding DRIFTIT-style arcade handling, top-down-first mobile presentation with optional chase camera, plus future JDM car collection and garage culture.

## Players
People who enjoy repeating short drift runs to master the throttle, handbrake, transitions, lines and lap times. Mobile is a first-class platform; PC and Xbox pad also matter.

## Core loop
Choose car → select circuit → three-lap drift run → improve time/score → earn credits (future) → tune or collect a car (future) → repeat.

## Locked principles
1. Handling must be challenging yet learnable, not auto-drifting.
2. A single original 2D planar physics model powers both cameras and all input devices.
3. Default camera high top-down as in DRIFTIT screenshots; behind-car is available on demand.
4. Visuals are clean 3D with a readable road, tyre marks, restrained smoke and Japanese drift atmosphere; do not introduce a photorealistic sim UI.
5. Mobile portrait HUD: time/lap top-left, score top-right, speed lower-left, virtual joystick lower-left, DRIFT/BRAKE/GAS on right; true multitouch.
6. Japanese fictional tracks inspired by the character, not copied geometry, of Ebisu/Nikko/Meihan.
7. NIGHTRIDEPL and Drift Games inspire car passion, garage and personalities only; no association, endorsement or licensed pack.
8. Separate approved-art locks: do not rework locked cars/visuals without new approval.

## Milestones
v0.1.0 Driving Lab: one fictional original track, one procedural car, physics lab, 3 laps, scoring, camera, PC/controller/mobile input code, headless validation.
v0.2.0 Competition: fully validated lap/checkpoint logic, time targets, ghost, combos and collisions.
v0.3.0 Circuits: three designed Japanese-style circuits and tuned day/night scenes.
v0.4.0 Dream Garage: owned cars, different physics profiles, cosmetics and parts, save migrations.
v0.5.0 Night Ride: night variants, headlight and audio polish, advanced lighting and optional nitro.
Later: potential creator collaboration packs subject to agreements and legal review.

## First-slice acceptance
Test a sustained sliding corner where gas holds the angle, steering can control it and lifting recovers grip. Input devices steer the same state machine; camera toggle is visual only. No instant finish-line exploits. All project scenes import and run in pinned Godot; physical device tests remain separate.

## Not in this slice
Copied source/assets from DRIFTIT, branded car packs, monetisation, massive open world or proprietary circuit reproduction.
