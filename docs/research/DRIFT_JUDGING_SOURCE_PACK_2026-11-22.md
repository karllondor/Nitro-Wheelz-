## A. VERIFIED DM-2026 QUALIFYING SCORING

Official solo qualifying maximum = **100 points** (sections 1.5–1.7):
| Category | Max | Official concept |
|---|---:|---|
| LINE | 60 | Follow the judges' designated line through **Outside Zones** and **Inside Clips**, potentially **Touch & Go** areas. Individual zone/clip allocations vary by event. Long Outside Zones can be split into successive scored subsectors. |
| ANGLE | 20 | Reach and maintain a high drift angle with sustained pace except where the judges mark deceleration or lower-angle allowance. Not merely steering-wheel angle. |
| STYLE: Initiation | 5 | Prompt, early entry with quick controlled arrival at target angle, with no unnecessary angle reductions/corrections. A 3-2-1 cone sequence marks the initiation context. |
| STYLE: Fluidity | 10 | Smooth, decisive transitions lock-to-lock, consistent course flow and correct acceleration/deceleration behaviour per the judges' map. |
| STYLE: Commitment | 5 | Consistent throttle, momentum and pace that fill zones and use road width decisively. |

**Official operational details:**
- Two nonconsecutive solo qualifying runs (section 1.5); this is a separate format from DTS OPEN's three runs.
- Two LINE judges assess the whole run; their line scores are averaged. Other category scores are added (the official PDF identifies judge roles and half-point scoring).
- Zone/clip *positions, shares of the 60 LINE points and target widths* are assigned at event briefing. **The rules do NOT prescribe a numerical universal metres-from-edge tolerance or an automatic zone-generation algorithm.** Never invent one as an 'official DM rule'.
- Figure 3a (PDF p.8) illustrates a map with START and FINISH, guide lines, scored OZ positions around multiple connected bends. Figure 3b shows an extended Outside Zone **OZ6** divided into four sections (x1, x2, x3, x4). REFERENCE THE ORIGINAL PAGE; do not duplicate the copyrighted artwork into the game or repo.

**Typical qualifying deductions (section 1.7, p.9):** double initiation, tyre beyond track, missed zones/clips, brief correction/straightening, departure from the target line, inadequate angle. The PDF illustrates categories but does not stipulate a universal numerical deduction table for each failure; per-event briefing determines judgement.

**Incomplete qualifying run examples (p.9):** spin; drift with wrong-direction angle for that segment; stop drifting; three wheels off the marked course (unless briefing exception); an unchaseable, badly flawed run. Open hood/doors also appear in the real regulations but need not be implemented without functioning damage/door physics. Do not automatically reduce every missed zone to zero: distinguish deductions from incomplete-run conditions.

**Intended reference for Nitro PRO:** 60/20/20 and original spatial intent of Outside Zone/Inside Clip/Touch & Go + long-zone sectors, not trademarked event assets, real judge discretion, identical league competition licensing, or multiplayer tandem.

## B. VERIFIED DTS-WINTER-2025/26 OPEN SCORING

The OPEN group is for less experienced drivers and drivers not wishing close tandem battles (DTS official 'O nas'). Relevant competition format, from p.5–6 (OPEN section):
- Solo judged runs; two training sessions precede OPEN competitive runs.
- Three individual scored qualifying runs.
- **0–100 points for each run**, with **arithmetic mean of the three** forming the OPEN result.
- Primary assessed qualities: **LINE, SPEED, ANGLE and STYLE**.
- The actual line, desired manner/style and detailed judging criteria are explained during the driver's **mandatory event briefing**.
- The PDF does **NOT** publish specific numerical shares (e.g. line 60 / angle 20 / style 20), exact clipping tolerances, default number/size of zones, or a universal rule for how many points each clip contributes. **Do not borrow DM's 60/20/20 breakdown and label it DTS.**
- The rules' FIGHTERS section concerns *tandem* matchups with 0/1/2 etc. points and zero-error examples such as spins, straightening, leaving asphalt, or contact with zone/clip markers. Those **FIGHTERS** rules must NOT silently be transferred to **OPEN**.

**Intended reference for Nitro CLUB:** accessible judged **solo** event driven by a human-style briefing/spec: line, speed, angle and style; three-run averaging can be shown as an optional game format. The owner wants Club zones to be wider/easier than Pro, but this tolerance difference is a **NITRO GAME DESIGN CHOICE**, not a published official DTS OPEN spatial requirement.

## C. SOURCE-TO-GAME TECHNICAL TRANSLATION — ORIGINAL PROPOSAL, NOT OFFICIAL RULES

1. **Street road styling is independent from judging:** selecting ROUTE in the existing Builder defaults to narrow ROAD/TOUGE with ordinary centre/edge lane paint; LOOP defaults to wide race track and kerbs. Public-road lane paint remains in PRACTICE. This is owner scope from Issue #29, not from either league.
2. **Practice** = no scored drift zones, no star awards. Ordinary ROAD centre and edge stripes stay.
3. **Club generator (DTS-informed)** = identify valid curved road segments and generate easier/forgiving target corridors and fewer scoring zones for accessible solo runs. Club score should track line, speed, angle and style while leaving final numerical weighting **clearly provisional**. These placements/widths are game heuristics, not official DTS geometry.
4. **Pro generator (DM-inspired)** = identify corner entry/apex/exit, signed turn direction, road-edge offsets and feasible drift path to place marked initiation, Outside Zones, Inside Clips, optional Touch & Go and transitions. Allocate *proposed* per-zone shares totaling the LINE 60 budget; angle 20; style 5+10+5. Split long zones when justified by geometry. No scoring zone on long STRAIGHTS. The official judge map is a human-picked example; do not claim a fixed one-to-one computer reconstruction.
5. **Core geometry** = curvature-smoothed road samples, signed heading change, turn arc length, width, local road edge/camber and bar/no-go clearance; use stable zone IDs from underlying segment IDs and deterministic regen after edits. No manual zone painting/moving/resizing UI. Owner edits road only.
6. **Vehicle feasibility** = verify both weaker and stronger cars can reach the corridor and clipping distance at reasonable approach/drift speed without crossing barriers, factoring yaw/slip, mass, torque, track width and transitions. If impossible, warn to redesign ROAD; don't silently alter authored roads.
7. **Actual scoring detection** = query car pose/footprint, direction of slide, slip angle, path sampled progression through scored zone intervals, continuity, off-road state, timestamps and control inputs. Avoid awarding angle points to straight driving or off-track proximity. Provisional tolerances for camera/mobile arcade handling are not published DM/DTS figures and need repeated tests.
8. **Rendering** = lightweight road-surface conforming asphalt paint, no floating overlays, no z-fighting. Real-world reference diagrams are about zone spatial placement, NOT mandatory reproduction of their exact colour palette, logos, protected artwork, or trade dress.
9. **Run data isolation** = current version v0.1.39 has three-star progression and saved results. Prototype Club/Pro scoring must be sandboxed from those records and does not award campaign stars. Do not claim 5-star system implemented.
10. **Review examples** (minimum): straight -> zero scored zones; a single left bend -> only relevant outside/inside zones; long sweeping bend -> appropriately extended/split sectors; S-bend -> correctly switched sides/transition; practise -> zero scored paint; Club/Pro -> distinct but both viable, including on narrow ROAD/TOUGE.
