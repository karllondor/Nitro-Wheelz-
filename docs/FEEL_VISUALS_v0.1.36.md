| 3 | "lighten the asphalt a bit" | asphalt tint 1.0 -> 2.1 (about twice as bright, neutral instead of blue-black) | `world/drift_track.gd` (`ASPHALT_TINT`) | `track_details_test` |

| 4 | "one clear NITRO Wheelz inscription in the middle of the asphalt on longer sections" | one painted inscription (crown + wordmark) on every straight >= 45 m, at most 4 per track, never within 28 m of the start line, inside the edge lines, letters stand up in the driving direction | `world/drift_track.gd` (`road_logo_spots`, `_build_road_logos`), `assets/brand/road_logo.png` | `track_details_test` |
| 5 | "advertising strip by the barriers and tiles on the concrete ... sense of scale and speed" | a band of printed boards on the inner face of both barriers (one mesh; our own names only: NITRO WHEELZ, DRIFT PARK, NW) and slab joints on the concrete shoulder of street circuits | `world/drift_track.gd` (`_build_barrier_ads`), `assets/surfaces/barrier_ads.png`, `concrete_slabs.png` | `track_details_test` |
| 6 | "tilted camera is nice, but the car must not move while the camera stands still" | NOT changed: the follow camera stays (a fixed camera would swap the joystick sides). The chase camera is already tilted (7-9.5 m high, 13-19 m back, looking 8 m ahead). Open: a DEVELOPER tilt slider if the phone test says more tilt is wanted | - | - |
| 7 | "angle and multiplier in one accent colour; does the angle count towards the score?" | Answer: yes, the angle is the biggest part of the score: points = speed x angle factor (0 below 10 deg, 0.25 -> 1.0 up to 25 deg, 1.0 -> 1.15 at 25 - 60 deg, falling to 0.3 above 60 deg) x zone x 12 per second; a spin (>= 115 deg) loses the chain. The multiplier (x1 .. x5) comes from time and flicks, not from the angle. No colour change in this version | `race/drift_scorer.gd` | `drift_scoring_test` (existing) |
