# Nitro Wheelz — visual identity (approved direction)

Default in-game presentation resembles the user's DRIFTIT mobile references: high near-orthographic top-down, clean readable asphalt, strong black skid marks, darkened translucent HUD panels, stylized low-poly colourful car, uncluttered outside scenery. Do not copy original textures, exact GUI, circuit map, name or licensed assets.

Second mode is a real 3D behind-car chase camera, toggleable without changing motion physics. Do not make chase camera mandatory or introduce an isometric static camera.

Identity: Japanese drift parks, JDM-shaped original low-poly cars, sunset, clean daylight, rain and eventually NIGHTRIDE-style night atmosphere. Cinematic HD-2.5D means physically 3D circuits and cars, crisp stylized/pixel-inspired surfaces, layered backgrounds and selective effects, not a high-fidelity racing simulator.

**First reference track:** Shin-Kawa Drift Park (fictional). Low-poly trees, concrete barriers, curbs, striped start line. Later: Akina Technical Loop, Kansai Wall Run, Midnight Touge, all original layouts.

**Portrait mobile:** lap/time panel top-left, score top-right, angle/speed lower-left, virtual analog steering left, GAS/BRAKE/DRIFT right. Design for simultaneous controls. PC UI adapts landscape.

**Future garage:** cars have distinct power/weight/wheelbase/steering profiles, personalities, audio and collectible status. Potential NIGHTRIDE and Drift Games licensed collaborations are speculative, not currently official.
