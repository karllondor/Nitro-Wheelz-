## Original generated assets (this repository)
- game/assets/surfaces/grass_night.webp, dirt_verge.webp, flower_cluster.png, blossom_leaf.png: generated by tools/generate_surface_textures.py (seeded numpy noise), no external images.
- game/assets/shaders/*.gdshader and all car/track/scenery geometry: written for Nitro Wheelz, procedural, no third-party meshes.
- game/assets/brand/nw_initials.png: the owner's NW initials logo, supplied in chat 2026-11-19 (cropped to the initials, transparent background). Owner artwork, not third-party. nw_initials_mask.png is a white silhouette of the same file (alpha kept) used for the engraved handbrake mark.
- game/assets/brand/app_icon*.png (v0.1.27 game icon): the owner's NW crown icon, supplied in chat 2026-11-20 (file 1000076932.png) and chosen by the owner as the game icon. Derived files only: white outer corners filled with the background colour (full-bleed 1024 / 192 px), adaptive Android foreground with the art scaled into the safe zone, solid background layer. Rights to the artwork are the owner's to confirm before a store release (the crown motif resembles well-known graffiti crowns).
- game/assets/shaders/studio_backdrop.gdshader: original garage backdrop shader.
- Car sound: generated at runtime by scripts/audio/engine_synth.gd (no samples). The owner's recorded SFX pack was removed in v0.1.21 (still in git history).
- game/assets/music/night_drift.ogg: original composition generated by tools/compose_music.py (no samples, no copied melody).
- RYU-32 coupe (scripts/vehicles/coupe_lowpoly.gd): original procedural model made from the owner's blue reference sheet (chat 2026-11-19); fictional name, no manufacturer badges or third-party meshes.
- E30 Blessed (game/assets/cars/e30_blessed/*.glb, v0.1.24): original mesh generated by tools/car_factory/blender/build_e30_blessed.py from the owner's DRAFT research package (assets/references/drafts/e30_blessed_v0.1/); shape of the real BMW E30 sedan reproduced from public factory dimensions and the supplied photos; no manufacturer badges, no creator logos or banners, blank plates. The reference photos are third-party and are NOT redistributed in this repository. In-game name "E30 BLESSED"; use of the NIGHTRIDE name/branding needs the creator's permission (README: no official partnership).
- Wheels wheel_signature_e30 / wheel_nw_deep_dish (game/assets/cars/wheels/): original meshes from tools/car_factory/blender/build_wheels.py; the Signature design is an approximation, not a copy of a branded wheel.
- **TEST ONLY, NOT FOR RELEASE** — game/assets/cars/ryu32_ai/* (v0.1.33, car "RYU-32 FLAME (AI TEST)"): body mesh and textures generated by the owner with an image-to-3D service on its FREE (non-commercial) plan from the owner's RYU-32 sheet; the texture contains an invented sponsor text and a fake plate. The owner accepted its use in this private test project (chat 2026-11-21) and will buy a paid plan only if the approach proves worthwhile. Must be regenerated under a commercial licence and cleaned (text, plates, marks, real-car likeness) before any publication. Processing: tools/car_factory/blender/import_ai_car.py; details in docs/car_factory/AI_BODY_PROTOTYPE.md.
- asphalt.webp and sand.webp remain the owner-supplied, source-derived textures from v0.1.11.

The starter intentionally has no blanket license. Choose an explicit license for code and a distinct policy for proprietary art before publication; no secrets/keystores in commits.
