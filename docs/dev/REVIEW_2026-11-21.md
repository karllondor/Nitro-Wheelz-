## Root cause evidence

**HUD:** `game/scripts/ui/race_hud.gd::_rebuild_outline()` unconditionally appends first point to the open track outline. `point_at_progress()` adds a fictitious last→first segment, and `_minimap()` infers start tangent from first/last points. Screenshot `1000077052.jpg` clearly shows resulting white diagonal. No runtime patch was made in this audit; issue #18 has proposed acceptance.

**Water:** `terrain_world.gd::_build_water()` builds the canyon stream at static `field.base_at()+1.0`, irrespective of the later road deformation and bridge deck. Intersections possible; geometry/elevation and actual point of overlap need reproduction. Don't assert this alone proves the user's specific screenshot cause.

**Wheels:** `glb_car.gd::set_motion()` yaws only `front_wheels`. That does not prove GLB mesh alignment, hub transforms, camber, mirroring and rotation are visually correct, particularly on first cold drift. Do not dismiss the issue based on 0.00-degree telemetry alone.

**Vegetation:** shader explicitly sways foliage `sin/cos(TIME)` with height weighting. Need inspect video at fixed camera to decide whether user reports excessive sway, flicker/instability or wants more lifelike movement; avoid silently disabling approved nature details.
