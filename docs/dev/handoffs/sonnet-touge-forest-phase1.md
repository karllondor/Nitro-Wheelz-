## Art direction / required improvements
- **Pine:** layered asymmetric green boughs, sturdy trunk, avoid stacked perfect cones.
- **Fir / Japanese cedar silhouette:** slimmer/vertical tree with staggered dense needles.
- **Birch:** narrow white bark with selective dark marks, delicate airy crown; recognizable even at low-poly scale.
- **Oak:** branched strong trunk, irregular large green canopy, green→gold/orange/russet autumn variants.
- **Sakura:** rounded airy cherry blossoms in spring, recognisable pink hue (avoid square/voxel-only blocks), sensible non-flower season.
- **Japanese maple:** spreading low branching, elegant red/orange/yellow foliage; not an opaque solid sphere.
- **Rowan:** slender branches, mixed green/yellow/red leaves, **small clustered red berries** (cheap shared meshes/vertex colours).
- **Shrubs:** low, varied shapes, some blossoms/berries and mossy green accents to fill the ground.
- Create 2–3 shape variants and palettes per species from shared meshes, seeded jitter. The approved illustration represents art direction; do not overfit triangle count or claim exact photoreal equivalence.
- Give Touge predominantly darker pine/cedar/fir, some bright birch/oak and a few Japanese maples as *intentional accents*. Pink sakura rare on mountain slopes unless specifically located as designed roadside scenic clusters. Autumn palette optional, not permanently red everywhere.
