# Nitro Wheelz v0.1.50 QA hotfix

Review base is PR #45 at `7865d419197a042d49f95e3b6114c57cd0ed86ae`. Branch `fix/qa-hotfix-v0150`, stacked on `feat/three-circuits-v0149`. Godot 4.7.2 stable, Compatibility renderer. Implementation by Codex. The workspace does not expose the exact serving model identifier.

## Changes

- #50: Practice, Club and Pro use the original full-size race results board. It shows banked points, run time, lap splits, best chain, transitions, kerb clips and lost chains. Club and Pro show filled, missed and unassessed sectors plus raw technical points before banking. Drive Again, Edit Road and Menu keep their existing actions. Ordinary results retain personal records and stars. Event outcomes still do not write records.
- #25 / #28: The existing upper GAS width now runs to the bottom. The outline and input share one rounded polygon in both orientations. Press feedback keeps the face stationary, so the hit area still matches the visible pedal. Each finger retains its own contact.
- #57: Inspection found two issues in the event paint path. A decal quad crossing the actual asphalt diagonal could cut across the two planes on a slope or curved, varying-width segment. Preset switching also replaced the unshaded paint material with a lit material on mesh vertices that had no normals. The physical-device recording also shows repeated parallel bands within otherwise intentional cyan and pink markings. Judged races now use a 320 m orthographic far plane and a 0.6 m chase near plane to give the 31 mm paint separation more depth precision. The paint batch clips to each asphalt plane, has explicit normals and retains its intentional palette under all four presets. Five successive Drive Again actions test the same paint mesh and clear run state, tyre marks and active score. The exact original handset streak has not been reproduced on physical hardware.
- #29: Wide CIRCUIT reserves part of the bend for side-to-side travel. The proposed Outside span is capped at 1.95× the original span, then shortened as needed for the bend, entry speed and transition. Club starts its Outside opportunity earlier where feasible. Pro can initiate before turn-in. Inside and Transfer timing can move deeper into a bend. Tight transfers can fall back to their proven earlier placement. The existing two-car feasibility check gates every proposed sector. ROAD follows the old generator and probe window. Sector score coefficients, award rules and save formats are unchanged.

See PR checks for captured images and pass/fail status. The signed review uses the prior PR #45 application ID, `com.karllondor.nitrowheelz.review.pr45`, with versionCode 50. ## Owner checklist

1. Finish a Fuji PRO and a Club run on your phone. Check score, time, sector counts and drift stats on the large panel. Try Drive Again, Edit Road and Menu. Also finish a Practice or standard run.
2. Hold steering, GAS and handbrake together. Use the lower-left side of GAS and release just that finger. Steering and handbrake should stay active.
3. On your saved circuit use the same car and viewpoint in DAWN, DAY, GOLDEN HOUR and NIGHT. Start five runs with Drive Again. Look for the streaks, especially on the same corner and in both cameras. The intended colour zones should still be present.
4. With the same car, drive the familiar ROAD HARD Club route and then its wide CIRCUIT equivalent. Note where Outside ends, whether the car has time to cross for Inside, and whether Transfer arrives at the moment you actually flick.
