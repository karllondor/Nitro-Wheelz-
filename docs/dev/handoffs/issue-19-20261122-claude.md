ROOT CAUSE (measured, not assumed)
`terrain_world.gd _build_water()` built the canyon stream once, at the channel ground + 1.0 m, before and independent of the road. The road solver puts the deck at the local terrain height and the terrain under a bridge is cut to 2.65 m below the deck, so over every bridge section the water was ABOVE or coplanar with the asphalt. Measured on the owner's world (Ojców seed 621727) and seed 2, four generated loops each: on 1108 of the 1113 road samples that lie over the ribbon the v0.1.33 water level was within 0.3 m of the asphalt or above it (worst 1.9 m above). In the real race scene at the crossing of a loop (route seed 3): water 0.63 / 0.79 / 1.06 m ABOVE the road surface (`evidence/issue-19-stream-before.txt`).

IMPLEMENTATION (`terrain_world.gd`)
- `_build_stream()` (called by `_build_water()` and after EVERY road change in `_set_road_begin`, i.e. edit / undo / redo / clear / load, so builder, race and reload agree) rebuilds the ribbon from `stream_level(lz)`.
- `stream_level()` = the natural level (channel ground + 1.0 m, unchanged) lowered wherever any part of the asphalt (+ 2 m margin) lies over the ribbon: held `STREAM_DECK_CLEAR_M = 2.2 m` below the road surface (the slab underside is 1.6 m below it, the carved bed 2.65 m), kept there `STREAM_PLATEAU_M = 4 m` along the stream (more than the sample spacing) and rising back at `STREAM_RAMP_SLOPE = 0.5` (short rapids, never a cliff). Ribbon vertices are placed on a 3 m grid plus every capped road sample so the lowest point is exact.
- Not changed: road heights, classes, bridges / piers, terrain meshes, scenery, physics. The stream is neither hidden nor flattened: away from crossings the level is the natural one (asserted).
