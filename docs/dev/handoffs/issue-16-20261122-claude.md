WHAT WAS CHECKED (in-engine, container, NOT the phone)
1. Wheel GEOMETRY (`wheel_smoke_test`, rest pose, 8 car configurations; numbers in `evidence/issue-16-wheel-geometry-rest-pose.txt`): left/right wheels mirror within 2 mm, front and rear tyres are the same size, axles sit on the catalog wheelbase (±3 cm), tyre bottoms touch the ground (±1.5 cm), outer faces stay inside the body width (+3 cm). In the first-drift capture (`capture_wheel_first_drift.gd`, both cameras, launch + handbrake flick) the rear wheel axle yaw is 0.00 deg, the front equals the steering angle, axle tilt equals the designed camber only (E30: ±2.5° front, ±4° rear). So no geometry / pivot / mirroring defect was found: the black shape is not a wheel mesh.
2. What else is drawn at exactly the rear-wheel positions during a drift: the tyre smoke emitters sit at `(±0.80, 0.30, 1.25)`. Reproduced in isolation (`capture_smoke_probe.gd`, `evidence/issue-16-smoke-probe.jpg`): a CPUParticles3D with the v0.1.33 smoke material (billboard with `billboard_keep_scale = false` + `vertex_color_use_as_albedo`) draws its YOUNGEST particles BLACK in a clean scene (soft round shape from the radial alpha, older puffs grey — the owner's picture), or opaque white in the race scene on llvmpipe (undefined colour = depends on driver / draw-order state). With `billboard_keep_scale = true` the same particles are the intended soft grey puffs. Matrix: keep_scale false -> black in both billboard modes; true -> fine.
3. The contact shadow and the skid ribbons were considered and not found to produce a disc with this look; the isolation toggle below settles it on the phone.
