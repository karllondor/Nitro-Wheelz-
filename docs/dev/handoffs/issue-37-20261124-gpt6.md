## Critical track blocker

The real **Fuji Speedway Small** (claimed ~1,286 m) has no identifiable
approved JSON, scene, geometry, or elevation data in the verified integration
repository (2026-11-24, complete recursive tree and code search). No matching
source asset was found by the available Google Drive search either.

The Visual Lab therefore runs on **SHIN-KAWA as an EXPLICIT PROXY** until
`game/tracks/fuji_speedway_small.json` is supplied and verified. This is
not a Fuji reconstruction or a Fuji QA claim. Never present proxy screenshots
as Fuji Speedway Small.

## Independent preview

Developer > VISUAL LAB. A standalone scene instantiates the existing race
unchanged. The lab snapshots and overrides per-instance materials, scene
environment and sun while preserving model physics, wheel pivots, shared GLB
resources, track mesh and collision. The original selected track setting is
restored immediately after race creation and upon exit.

* Vehicle A: duplicated existing premium die-cast material, controlled
  roughness/gloss and no geometry change.
* Vehicle C: original factory paint, variant selection and livery/atlas UVs
  sampled by lightweight editorial illustrated shader.
* Environment A: mattified original material resources without changing road.
* Environment C: texture/vertex colour preserving two-tone material
  overrides. Source decals, glass, water, shadows and tyre effects remain
  unchanged to protect alpha blending and live dynamics.
* Day, Sunset and Night: procedural sky and one existing directional key,
  with all light shadows disabled for mobile compatibility.
* Two real SpotLight3D headlights attached to the visual car node; visible
  red rear position lamps, stronger brake/handbrake lamps and reverse lamps.
  Position is approximate for the placeholder vehicle, not AE86 validated.
* Non-colliding round-canopy Sakura/pines and simple containers are
  experimental preview-only scenery.
* Open panel shows FPS, approximate FPS-derived frame budget and draw calls.
  Screenshot and 24-view matrix capture write to `user://visual_lab/`.
* Restore original materials and scene lighting, reset lab style, or exit.
