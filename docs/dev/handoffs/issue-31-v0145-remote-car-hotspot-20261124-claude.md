WHY: owner QA result above. A multiplayer prototype that makes the first two-phone session feel glitchy at the start, and that hides its offline mode, fails its own purpose.

ROOT CAUSE: **UNCONFIRMED.** Nothing in the v0.1.44 receiver is obviously wrong in isolation, so I built a deterministic model of two phones and a link (see TESTS) and looked for conditions under which the old receiver visibly pops. Findings in the model: (1) a link stall of 0.4–0.6 s (Wi-Fi scan / roaming / power-save) makes it extrapolate for 250 ms, stand, then jump to the real position (up to 4.6 m in one frame); (2) on 30 fps phones the snapshots were stamped with the wall clock of the frame a physics tick ran in, so a 33 ms frame quantisation turned into uneven apparent car speed (13 frames > 0.5 m); (3) after a sender hitch the Hermite tangents were stretched over the long span (overshoot). Bursty delivery and ordinary spikes alone did NOT produce visible pops in the old receiver in this model. Whether any of this is what the owner saw is not known; the `LINK` word and a screenshot from the next test are meant to tell.

IMPLEMENTATION: see `docs/LOCAL_MULTIPLAYER.md` section "v0.1.45". Short: `NitroNetRemoteView` = steered render clock (±15 %), adaptive delay 110–340 ms from the peak age of the newest snapshot (per frame, so the phone's own hitches do not count as jitter), discontinuity smoothing (error bled off over ~0.1 s; snap only on a deliberate reset or > 6 m), clock-step guard (re-sync after half a second of snapshots > 0.7 s late), same interpolation / ≤ 250 ms extrapolation / refusal of impossible jumps as before. Lobby: gold "NO WI-FI? NO PROBLEM. ONE PHONE'S HOTSPOT IS ENOUGH." line, a NO WI-FI? HOTSPOT HOW-TO page with the steps for both phones, rewritten no-address / not-on-a-network / unreachable messages.

TESTS (container, headless, Godot 4.7.2):
* `net_remote_view_test` (NEW): unit behaviour + 7 scenarios x 5 seeds x 15 s of simulated drive, the SAME packets through a faithful copy of the v0.1.44 receiver and the new one. Result: v0.1.44 receiver 21 visible pops (> 0.5 m in one frame, worst 4.60 m, all in the link-stall and 30 fps scenarios); new receiver 0 pops, worst 0.35 m per frame, mean display lag 68–139 ms (worst single frame 346 ms in a 0.6 s stall). Trade-off made explicit: the new receiver can be up to 0.3 m off the true path while it bleeds an error away, and trails the real car by its adaptive delay. The test fails if the model stops reproducing the old jump (guard against a test that proves nothing). Mutation check: with the clock-step guard disabled the sender-stop unit test fails (car drawn 3.6 m ahead of its data).
* `multiplayer_lobby_test` (extended): visible hotspot line, both phones' steps, BACK returns to the menu, no networking started by reading the how-to. `net_race_test` (extended): the other player's label carries the points and the LINK word. `net_session_test`, `net_race_test`, `multiplayer_lobby_test` PASS on the real ENet loopback.
